# Privacy baseline

HOF processes public social metadata and campus recognition information. Publicly accessible information is not automatically unrestricted for republication. Follow platform terms and applicable law. Do not scrape private content or bypass controls.

Collect only what is necessary. Keep identity/campus evidence private and access-controlled. Do not expose submitter identity, voter identity, private moderation notes or evidence references publicly. Define retention, correction, report and deletion workflows before launch. Treat avatar URLs from providers as potentially temporary.
